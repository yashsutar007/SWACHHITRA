Place a pin to check whether a supported Ward boundary covers this location.
Ward detection limitation

Ward assignment is only as reliable as the boundaries currently saved in SWACHHITRA. Application-defined and development polygons are not verified official KMC Ward boundaries. If no saved polygon covers the pin, the address is saved but the Ward remains unconfirmed, and Ward-routed complaint submission will stay unavailable.
