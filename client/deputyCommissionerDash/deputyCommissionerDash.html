PART 6 · ACCESS GOVERNANCE

Security & Administration

Review staff access and apply controlled account-status changes with a required reason and a recorded audit trail.

These controls manage only Sanitary Inspector and Assistant Commissioner accounts. Citizen accounts, Deputy Commissioner accounts, your own account, roles, passwords and operational records cannot be changed here. Deactivating or reactivating an eligible account invalidates its saved sessions.
Managed staff accounts—Assistant Commissioners and Inspectors
Active accounts—Can sign in when other access checks pass
Inactive accounts—Rejected by the current authentication middleware
Active Assistant Commissioners—The final active AC is protected from deactivation
Active Inspectors—Current saved account statuses
Incomplete operational scope—Missing Division or required Ward assignment
